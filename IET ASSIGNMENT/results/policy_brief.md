# Policy Brief: Climate-Resilient Crop Yield Forecasting

The integrated pipeline combines historical climate/soil preparation, instance-based kNN, locally weighted regression, and discretised version-space analysis. Validation selected k=21 with Mahalanobis distance and tau=2.0 for LWR on the included reproducibility benchmark.

## Policy implications
1. Use forecasts as decision support, not automatic planting instructions.
2. Prioritise local weather and soil monitoring where subgroup error is higher.
3. Reassess models as climate distributions shift beyond the historical training range.
4. Report uncertainty and avoid a single forecast threshold for all regions or crops.

## SDG linkage
Climate-aware planning can support food availability and resilience (SDG 2) while encouraging adaptation to climate variability (SDG 13).
