# Task F Policy Brief

## What the benchmark indicates
The integrated pipeline combines historical climate/soil preparation, instance-based kNN, locally weighted regression, and a discretised version-space analysis. Validation selected k=21 with the mahalanobis distance and tau=2.0 for LWR on the included reproducibility benchmark.

## Policy implications
1. Use yield forecasts as decision support, not as automatic planting instructions.
2. Prioritise local weather and soil monitoring where subgroup error is higher.
3. Reassess models as climate distributions shift beyond the historical training range.
4. Report uncertainty and avoid using a single forecast threshold for all regions or crops.

## SDG linkage
Better climate-aware planning can support food availability and resilience (SDG 2) while encouraging adaptation to climate variability (SDG 13).
